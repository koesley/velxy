# V57.3 Omniverse Intelligence Council

Adds workspace-isolated investigation cases anchored to Living Business Graph entities, deterministic four-perspective investigation prompts, evidence and challenge notes, owner-only review decisions, audit events, JSON overview and responsive UI.

Launch local SQLite studio with VELXTRIA_LOCAL_STUDIO=1 and VELXTRIA_DEPLOYMENT_MODE=local, then visit /sovereign-council-v57.

## Limits
Generated prompts are NOT independent AI agents, verified findings, forecasts or automatically executed actions. Manual evidence references require verification. Investigations are capped at 100 in the overview. The UI has not been production-browser tested.
